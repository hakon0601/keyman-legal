Keyman Privacy Policy

Effective date: 18 September 2026 · Applies to the Keyman iOS app and related Keyman software you choose to run.

Experimental software. Keyman is a highly experimental voice / LLM assistant. There is no guarantee of confidentiality, correctness, safety, or security. Using it means you accept that prompts, audio, and tool actions may leave your device and may be processed by systems we do not control (cloud LLM vendors, your own Keyman box, Homebridge, and other services you configure).

1. Who we are

Keyman is developed by Håkon Gimse (“we”, “us”). This policy describes how the Keyman iOS app (com.keyman.app) handles information, and how that relates to optional Keyman server software (for example on a Raspberry Pi) and third-party AI providers.

We do not operate a Keyman cloud that receives your chats by default. Most processing is on your phone, on hardware you run, or at vendors you connect with your own API keys.

2. What Keyman can access

Depending on features you enable, the app may use:

  • Microphone — wake word listening, voice commands, speech-to-text.
  • Local network — to reach a Keyman box you configure on your LAN or Tailscale.
  • Background audio — so playback / listening can continue briefly while the screen is locked.
  • On-device storage — conversation history, preferences, downloaded models.
  • Keychain — API keys and invite codes you paste in (never logged by the app).
  • Clipboard — only if you copy message text yourself.
  • Internet — model downloads (e.g. Hugging Face), cloud LLM APIs, and your Keyman server.

The app requests the permissions needed for these features. Granting a permission does not mean we “collect everything for advertising”; it means the feature can run. You can deny microphone or local network access and use text-only / on-device modes where available.

3. Data the app handles

  • Audio — recorded for wake word and transcription. On-device Whisper processes audio on the phone when that mode is selected. If you use a Keyman box with online or server STT, audio or transcripts may be sent to that box (and onward to whatever STT provider the box is configured to use).
  • Chat content — messages, tool results, and conversation history stored in the app’s Documents area. Content you send in Sky modes goes directly from the phone to OpenAI, Anthropic, or xAI using your key. Content you send in server modes goes to your Keyman box, which may call cloud LLMs, home automation, calendars, search, and other tools.
  • Preferences & identifiers — LLM/STT/TTS settings, server addresses, tool toggles, and a random client id in UserDefaults (so the box can keep per-client settings).
  • Secrets you supply — cloud API keys and invite codes in Keychain; they are sent only to the corresponding provider or your box when you use that feature.
  • Sensitive information — we do not ask for health, financial, or government data. Anything you type or say into an LLM can still include sensitive content. Treat every conversation as potentially recorded and potentially forwarded.

4. What we (the developers) do not do

  • We do not sell personal data.
  • We do not use your chats for advertising or cross-app tracking (no ATT / IDFA tracking).
  • We do not operate analytics SDKs that phone home from the iOS app for marketing.
  • We cannot see Keychain values on your phone, and we do not receive your cloud API keys through the Keyman box in Sky mode.

5. Third parties and systems we do not control

When you enable them, data may be processed by:

  • OpenAI, Anthropic, xAI (Sky) — subject to their privacy policies and retention.
  • Your Keyman server — often has no authentication on the LAN / Tailscale path; anyone who can reach it can use its tools. Transcripts and earshot memory may be stored in plaintext on that machine.
  • Homebridge and other home / web services configured on the box.
  • Hugging Face (or similar) when downloading on-device models.
  • Apple — standard OS processing (speech synthesis voices, Keychain, etc.).

LLM providers and a compromised or misconfigured box may log, train on, leak, or mishandle prompts. We accept no responsibility for what those systems do.

6. Children

Keyman is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age in your jurisdiction). Do not use it to process children’s data if applicable law forbids it.

7. Retention and deletion

  • On the phone: delete conversations in the app, or delete the app to remove local Documents / preferences (Keychain items may survive reinstall on the same Apple ID — that is iOS behaviour).
  • On your box: you control conversations/, logs, and related files; wipe them on the device.
  • At cloud vendors: follow each vendor’s account / data tools; we cannot delete data held by them.

8. International transfers

If you use US-based LLM APIs or download models from abroad, your data leaves your country under those providers’ terms. We do not offer a separate transfer mechanism.

9. No warranty; experimental risk

Keyman is provided as is. Features may record more than you expect, send data to the wrong device, trigger smart-home actions, run coding agents, or produce harmful LLM output. There is inherited, irreducible risk from connecting language models to tools and microphones. By using Keyman you acknowledge that risk and that we make no guarantee about privacy, safety, or fitness for any purpose — especially not medical, legal, financial, or life-critical use.

10. Changes

We may update this policy in the repository. The “Effective date” at the top will change. Continued use after an update means you accept the revised policy.

11. Contact

Questions about this policy: open an issue on the public repository github.com/hakon0601/keyman-public (do not paste secrets, API keys, or real transcripts into issues).
